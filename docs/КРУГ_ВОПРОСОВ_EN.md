## 1. Three scales of questions — and they must not be confused

The questions of the Human live in three different scales. Mixing scales is the main source of confusion: a substitution of level looks like an argument, while in fact the parties talk about different things.

| Scale | What it is | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **The individual** | what matters to a specific person — each has their own circle | a favourite song, one's own voice, one's own comfort, one's own boundaries and consent |
| **Communities** | what people need for a shared life: coordination, communication, organisation, conflicts, justice and the like | hall ·18, the circle of one's own ·23, connection ·22, the evening console ·31, the order of the fond ·33 |
| **The Human as a whole** | questions that exist **independently of a specific individual and are not the sum of the interests of the majority**; they are about the conditions of existence, the structure and development of the Human in general | what existence is, reality and truth, what the Human is, life, safety, environment, resources |

The House lives in all three scales at once and keeps them separate: the people matrix ·31 is the scale of the individual, halls and connection are the scale of communities, and this document is the scale of the Human as a whole.

## 2. The general circle of questions — a frame about the Human as a whole

**The general circle of questions is not «the list of what worries everyone» and not «the sum of individual interests». It is a frame of questions about the Human as a whole.**

The question «does this worry everyone» belongs to the scale of the individual or of communities. The general circle is not counted by a vote: a question about what truth is, or what safety is, does not become a topic — or stop being one — depending on how many people are thinking about it.

From the owner's words — the circle itself:

- **Existence** — what existence is, reality and truth, what the Human is, life, safety, environment, resources.
- **Interaction** — how interaction, freedom, communication, organisation, conflicts work.
- **Cognition** — how cognition, consciousness, information, learning, science, technology, creativity, evolution work.
- **Movement** — where everything is heading; what values, ethics, justice, continuity, the future and meaning are.
- **Universal capabilities** — discovering the unknown, self-organising, adapting, self-developing and creating new possibilities of existence.

## 3. The circle is open — mechanisms for discovering new questions

**This list is not closed.** The circle must contain mechanisms for discovering questions that did not exist before — otherwise the circle freezes and turns into dogma.

How the House opens new questions:

- **The «WHAT IS MISSING» method** ([ДОКУМЕНТЫ/ПОЛИМАТ_ЧЕГО_НЕТ/РАЗБОР_ЧЕГО_НЕТ.md](../ДОКУМЕНТЫ/ПОЛИМАТ_ЧЕГО_НЕТ/РАЗБОР_ЧЕГО_НЕТ.md)) — «I am interested in what is NOT there»: a harsh comparison of the declared with the real; the hole left after it is a new question.
- **Door ·35 QUESTIONS** — the House's living list of questions with honest answers; a question to which the House answers «honestly pointing at the door where it lives» is a signal of a growing circle.
- **Revision and live verification** (ЭП-06, the truth shop, guards) — verification does not let any answer freeze: «don't trust — verify».
- **The owner's word** (HUMAN = ACCOUNT = PID 1) — the owner puts a question into the circle, as was done with this document; without a human's word the circle does not expand silently.

## 4. Three fundamental directions

From the open circle — three directions along which everything the House does lives:

1. **Understand the existing.** Study what is: canons, laws, texts, the world around. In the House — documentation, law codices, architecture, the dictionary ·36.
2. **Discover the possible.** Find what is not yet in the picture: the «WHAT IS MISSING» method, the reconnaissance of world practices, revisions, live verification.
3. **Create what does not yet exist.** Build what is absent from the world: new doors, new capabilities, new environments of existence.

It is the third direction that changes the construction: **the Human does not merely study the world and adapt — the Human creates an environment and new possibilities of existence.**

## 5. The Human's role in the noosphere

The noosphere is the environment of questions and meanings in which humanity lives; it is held not by a machine and not by a state, but by the Human.

- **The Human is the one who asks questions.** A machine answers, searches, counts and builds; the Human poses the question. In the House this is wired in as a law: HUMAN = ACCOUNT = PID 1 ([ДОКУМЕНТЫ/ЧЕЛОВЕК_И_ИНДИВИД.md](../ДОКУМЕНТЫ/ЧЕЛОВЕК_И_ИНДИВИД.md)) — the decree is primary, executors are secondary.
- **The Human is the one who creates the new.** Understanding the existing and discovering the possible are the Human's hands; creating what does not yet exist is the Human's role in the noosphere: not adaptation to an environment, but the building of environments and new possibilities of existence.
- **The House is an instrument of that role.** SINGULAR is a communicator of the Human: an offline house where song, word and knowledge are available to everyone, without servers above one's head and without someone else's will between the human and the deed. Every beat of the House is movement along the three directions: understand — discover — create.
- **No one replaces the Human.** Neither a vote of machines, nor a sum of interests, nor an «advanced majority» poses questions for the Human: the circle is common, and the one who decides is the Human (THE HUMAN DECIDES).
