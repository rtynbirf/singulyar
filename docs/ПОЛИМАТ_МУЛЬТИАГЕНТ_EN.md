## 1. What «polymath = multi-agent» means

One agent cannot do everything at once and should not: long pipelines (pushes, verifications, test runs) and independent work branches (reconnaissance, stands, packages) are done in parallel — by subagents and parallel sessions. But a swarm without law becomes a crowd: two agents take the same walk, overwrite each other's bytes, lose the chronicle. The house answers with law: **the swarm is an orchestra, not a bazaar**. Each agent enters the tact (the ritual), plays its part (the brief) and appends the shared chronicle without rewriting anyone else's.

## 2. Eight laws of the swarm (with house precedents)

**L.1 · ONE KEY PER TACT.** Every walk uses one key, named in the dossier; a second key only by the owner's word. *Precedent: PAT №26 → №27 → №28 rotated through tacts v11.16 → v11.23 → v11.27, each inscribed into the map.*

**L.3 · DIRECTIVE 2 — strictly in order.** Finish one point completely, then the next; do not jump from place to place. *Precedent: Ф.1 «forced tail-squeezing» goes one per tact: ПВ2 (C108) → ПВ3 (C109, this tact).*

**L.4 · The chronicle is append-only.** The chronicle in [versions.json](../data/versions.json) and [worklog.md](../worklog.md) is only appended: others' entries stay byte-for-byte, a new entry lands at the end. *Precedent: 117 chronicle entries, each verified by the append-only gate on every push.*

**L.5 · Don't touch colleagues' bytes; collisions — honest renumbering.** If a parallel session's walk lands between your dossier and your push — their walk is accepted in full, yours is honestly renumbered, colleagues' bytes untouched. *Precedents: C80 (their ДУЭТ = my v11.03), C99, C106–C108 (double renumbering v11.28→v11.29→v11.30 in one tact).*

## 3. The swarm's hands and handover

A subagent does not see the owner's conversation — it sees a **brief**. So a brief must be self-sufficient: the task, the boundaries, what not to touch, what to return. The swarm's shared journal is `worklog.md`: every agent reads others' entries before work and appends its own after (template: Task ID, agent, task, work log, stage summary). The lenses of the MANY-LEARNED method ([ПОЛИМАТ_ПРОТОКОЛ](ПОЛИМАТ_ПРОТОКОЛ.md)) remain the main content filter; the stages of a walk — [РИТУАЛ_ТАКТА](РИТУАЛ_ТАКТА.md); the boundaries of what is allowed — [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).

## 5. The boundary of honesty

Multi-agency does not cancel responsibility: whoever pushes is accountable (their key, their STOP-HORN, their chronicle). A subagent does not push to main. An agent does not invent precedents: if there is none — honestly «none». This law is not a report about the past but a working discipline: every next tact passes through it just as through the ritual.
