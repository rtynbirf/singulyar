# ADR-0001: Box isolation of function families (script blocks)

**Status:** ✅ Accepted (2026-10) · **Tacts:** v10.38–v10.49 · **Updated:** 2026-10-11

## Context

The House is dozens of function families (voice, scene, light, tact-pulse, console) living in one site with no bundler. `window.*` globals collide: different families write the same name, the init order is fragile, editing one family breaks its neighbours. A bundler is against the House canon: zero dependencies, zero build.

## Decision

Each family lives in its own **box** — an isolated `<script>` block (an IIFE/module block with a Cyrillic number С23…С29). Inside the box — its own scope; outward the family is exposed by a single governed stack of bridges (see ADR-0002). The common wardrobe is `window.__КОМОД` (the registry of families, kept by the TREASURER since v10.37).

## Consequences

- ✅ The families don't gnaw each other: a global conflict is structurally impossible.
- ✅ Zero bundler: the files are edited and shipped by tacts as they are.
- ⚠️ The load order of the boxes is critical — the bridges hold the relocation order.
- ⚠️ Debugging through globals requires knowing which family trades what (the TREASURER registry + the ZDOROVIE health probes).
