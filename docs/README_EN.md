## Rules of this folder

- One fact lives in one place: the road — in the ROADMAP, facts about the released — in the CHANGELOG, the truth about the structure — in ARCHITECTURE.
- The header of each document answers: status, version, date. At the bottom — neighbours.
- Privacy is law: only what can be shown to the world is published in `docs/`. Personal data, keys, internal journals — outside the repo.
- Door counters are taken from `data/versions.json` and `singulyar-modules.js` (the core manifest) — not from old paragraphs of the README.
