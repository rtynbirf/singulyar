## What it means

**POLYMATH = MANY-SCHOLAR** is not a title or a compliment — it is a **working method**: no task of the home is solved by a single discipline. A narrow specialist sees every problem as a nail, because the only tool in hand is a hammer. The many-scholar first asks: "what IS this problem?" — and only then picks instruments, taking them from several workshops at once. World history confirms it: Leonardo studied anatomy to draw, and drew to understand anatomy; Lomonosov wrote verse to explain physics; von Neumann designed machines through mathematics and pushed mathematics through machines. Synthesis is not an add-on to specialization — it is a separate skill.

For the SINGULAR home this is literal: the home stands at the crossroads of disciplines — karaoke (culture), sound (acoustics), 200 languages (linguistics), a blind owner (pedagogy of accessibility), offline with no server (engineering and law), a chronicle (history). The home itself is a polymath's artifact.

## The law of the MANY-SCHOLAR — five rules

1. **Synthesis over specialization.** "Not my field" is forbidden: the field is the whole home. If a discipline is not your strength — take the world's best practice (see [ЛУЧШИЕ_ПРАКТИКИ](ЛУЧШИЕ_ПРАКТИКИ_EN.md)) and verify it.
2. **Every work passes at least three lenses.** A problem solved through one discipline is unsolved: it will break the home in another discipline. Three is the minimum of honesty; twelve is the maximum.
3. **Every lens is verifiable.** ANTI-HALLUCINATION mode (a law of the home): a fact is either verified (a source, a probe, a live measurement) or marked "UNVERIFIED". No beautiful guesses without the mark.
4. **Depth in one discipline does not buy honesty in the others.** An elephant is described whole, not trunk-only: tested code unreadable by the blind owner — fail; beautiful text with lying numbers — fail (Н2 «ПРАВДА» proved it).
5. **The polymath's result is a home artifact, not an opinion.** A protocol, a script, a track canon, a map entry. The home does not remember talk without artifacts.

## Five steps of the method

1. **Break the task into the language of lenses.** "Playback files get evicted" is not one problem but four: storage (engineering), rights (law), weight (mathematics), trust (chronicle).
2. **Choose the lenses honestly.** Not all twelve — three to five per task. A lens picked for beauty is ballast; a lens dropped out of laziness is a future bug.
3. **Ask the world and the home.** The world — best practices (the advisors, [ЛУЧШИЕ_ПРАКТИКИ](ЛУЧШИЕ_ПРАКТИКИ_EN.md)); the home — maps, track canons, the chronicle. The world answers with sources, the home with precedents.
4. **Synthesize into an artifact.** Code, document, track canon, map entry. The artifact passes the full tact ritual: patcher → gates → force:false push → live verification.
5. **Verification first, fact second.** Only after probes and the live ЭП-06 check does a map line close. Without verification it stays "🚧 in work", however pretty.
