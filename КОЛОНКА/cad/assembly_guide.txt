OMNISPEAKER-1 Mechanical Assembly & 3D Printing Guide

1. 3D Printing Settings:
   - Body & Chamber: PETG, 0.2mm layer height, 4 perimeter walls, 35% Gyroid infill.
   - Passive Radiator Membrane: TPU 95A, 100% infill, 2 perimeter walls.
   - Acoustic Cone Reflector: PETG/PLA+, 0.16mm layer height, smooth surface ironing enabled.

2. Assembly Steps:
   a. Install TAS5805M amplifier and Tang Band 3" speaker into chamber using butyl acoustic sealant.
   b. Mount TPU passive radiator to side 85mm opening using M3 screws and gasket.
   c. Insert silicone O-rings (8x) into microphone top ring holes and seat XMOS XU316 board.
   d. Secure 45-degree acoustic dispersion cone above speaker driver.
   e. Connect HLK-LD2450 mmWave sensor to internal UART mounting bracket.
